ENERGY MODEL

P_cpu = P_idle + (P_max - P_idle) × cpu_fraction

P_total = P_cpu + P_ram + P_disk

Energy = (P_total × time_s) / 3,600,000 kWh

CO₂ = Energy × CarbonIntensity (gCO₂/kWh)

Desktop Mid Profile: 12W idle → 65W max TDP

RAM: 0.375W per GB (16GB = 6W)

SSD: 2W constant

RATING SCALE
🌿 MINIMAL < 0.01g CO₂
✅ LOW 0.01 — 0.1g CO₂
⚠️ MEDIUM 0.1 — 1.0g CO₂
🔥 HIGH 1.0 — 10g CO₂
💀 CRITICAL > 10g CO₂
ML PIPELINE

Algorithm: Random Forest Regressor

Features: executionTime, cpuUsage,
